Hackathon Submission

Veyronis

Cross-chain escrow and dispute resolution using Attestcoin-verified evidence on Creditcoin.

Veyronis keeps funds self-custodial on Ethereum Sepolia while using Creditcoin-attested source blocks and Attestcoin verification to authorize evidence-based escrow conditions.

Built by Ogar Emma · Ethereum Sepolia · Creditcoin testnet
01
Problem

Cross-chain agreements create a trust gap

Proof lives on another chain

The obligation may be proven by a Sepolia transaction, while the escrow and payout rules live with a separate agreement contract.

Traditional escrow cannot verify it

An escrow agent can hold funds, but cannot independently authenticate a transaction on another blockchain or validate its context.

Manual review is fragile

Screenshots and pasted hashes can be copied, replayed, taken out of context, or attached to the wrong agreement.

Intermediaries add friction

Marketplaces and centralized custodians introduce custody risk, delays, fees, and opaque dispute decisions.

02
Solution

Self-custodial escrow with verifiable evidence

Veyronis separates custody from verification: the escrow controls funds, while Attestcoin and Creditcoin verify the cross-chain fact that satisfies the agreement condition.

Buyer-controlled funding

A dedicated VeyronisEscrow contract holds the exact required amount on Ethereum Sepolia.

Immutable evidence policies

Each agreement commits to expected source chain, transaction context, participants, asset, amount, and evidence rules.

Verified source facts

Proof Builder and Creditcoin BlockProver verify transaction inclusion after the source block is attested.

Authorized settlement

EvidenceClaimRegistry records the verified claim and authorizes the escrow condition; it does not directly transfer funds.

03
How It Works

From source transaction to seller withdrawal

Step 01Source transaction
Step 02Attestcoin validation
Step 03Creditcoin source-block attestation
Step 04Proof Builder
Step 05Creditcoin BlockProver
Step 06Attested transaction decoding
Step 07Evidence-policy validation
Step 08EvidenceClaimRegistry
Step 09Escrow condition settlement
Step 10Seller withdrawal

A verified claim satisfies the escrow condition. The escrow then credits the seller's internal withdrawal balance. The seller signs a separate withdrawal transaction; evidence verification never directly pays the wallet.

04
USC / Creditcoin Integration

Creditcoin verifies. Sepolia holds custody.

Attestcoin / USC validation

Veyronis validates the configured Sepolia source-chain context before requesting proof material.

Creditcoin source-block attestation

The transaction's source block must be attested before Veyronis accepts proof material.

Proof Builder

Proof Builder generates the transaction inclusion proof from the attested source block.

Creditcoin BlockProver

BlockProver verifies transaction inclusion and attested block continuity through the USC SDK.

Creditcoin supplies the verification layer. Escrow funds remain on Ethereum Sepolia in VeyronisEscrow. Attestcoin does not directly transfer escrow funds, and Creditcoin does not hold them.
05
Architecture

A separation of custody, metadata, and proof

Frontend

Next.js wallet workflows for agreement creation, funding, verification, disputes, withdrawal, and status.

Backend

Native Node.js/TypeScript service for Attestcoin orchestration, proof coordination, policy checks, API, and reconciliation.

PostgreSQL

Metadata, evidence references, and verification records. Never authoritative for balances or settlement.

Ethereum Sepolia

Chain ID 11155111 hosts VeyronisEscrow and EvidenceClaimRegistry custody and settlement state.

Creditcoin & Attestcoin

Chain ID 102031 provides source-block attestation, Proof Builder integration, and BlockProver verification.

Contract boundary

VeyronisEscrow controls custody and withdrawal credits; EvidenceClaimRegistry binds verified evidence to the agreement.

07
Use Cases & Future

Evidence-based conditional payouts

Cross-chain commerce

Release escrow when a payment or delivery condition is independently verified on the source chain.

Milestone payments

Bind each payout to a source-chain event and release only after the committed evidence policy passes.

Contributor escrow

DAOs and protocols can condition contributor payouts on verifiable on-chain milestones.

Service agreements

Use evidence policies for cross-chain service obligations and conditional settlement.

Dispute resolution

Record verified evidence as an advisory input while retaining an arbitrator for subjective judgment.

Prototype direction

Future work can broaden supported source events and automate safe retry orchestration without changing custody boundaries.

Closing

Trust the evidence. Keep custody self-custodial.
